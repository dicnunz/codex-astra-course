Paper 3

1,000-1,200 words. Due in Session 18.

Choose one question. State the claim you are evaluating precisely. Reconstruct the strongest relevant reasoning from the assigned sources, defend your own position, address a serious objection, and use concrete Codex behavior or a proposed test where it bears on the argument.

  1. Astra can preserve notes across context windows while earlier context remains searchable. Explain what problem this addresses, and argue for what information should still be written into durable project files rather than left in conversational history.
  2. Analyze debugging as an evidence problem. Starting from a reproducible failure, explain why a patch plus a passing test can still be insufficient. Give a stronger sequence that rules out at least two plausible false victories.
  3. Kundel describes goal prompts as exit criteria for long-running work. What properties make an exit criterion safe and useful? Consider measurable targets, proxy metrics, stopping conditions, and the risk of optimizing the wrong quantity.
  4. Write on any unused topic from Paper 2, but substantially revise the question by incorporating long-context behavior, goals, or evidence from Sessions 14-17.

Citations

Cite documentation, employee writing, and any run evidence closely enough that another reader could locate it. Do not submit agent-generated prose as your own analysis.
