Paper 1

1,000-1,200 words. Due in Session 8. A revised version is due in Session 12.

Choose one question. State the claim you are evaluating precisely. Reconstruct the strongest relevant reasoning from the assigned sources, defend your own position, address a serious objection, and use concrete Codex behavior or a proposed test where it bears on the argument.

  1. Reconstruct and assess the claim that a strong Codex task should be defined primarily by a verifiable finish condition rather than by a long sequence of instructions. Use the Astra guidance and Kundel on goals or verification. Give a case where the finish condition is enough, and a case where it is not.
  2. Kundel reports that Astra can do more product thinking when given useful references and relevant work. Explain the strongest version of that recommendation. Then argue for a boundary between details the agent should infer and details the user should specify.
  3. OpenAI recommends auditing skills and AGENTS.md because Astra follows instructions more strongly. Is instruction sensitivity mainly a capability gain or a new source of failure? Defend a position using at least two concrete instruction conflicts.
  4. When choosing reasoning effort, should the default policy be to begin at Low or Medium and escalate only after evidence of need? State a decision rule, give an objection based on difficult tasks, and explain what observation would justify escalation.

Citations

Cite documentation, employee writing, and any run evidence closely enough that another reader could locate it. Do not submit agent-generated prose as your own analysis.
