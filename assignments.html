Assignments

Papers

In this class you will write three 1,000-to-1,200 word papers and one 2,000-to-2,400 word final paper, for a minimum of 5,000 words. You will revise and resubmit your first paper after meeting with your recitation instructor.

For details, use the paper links in the course menu.

General Guidelines

Things that make for a good paper are: clear and accurate reconstructions of claims or workflow arguments from the readings and lecture; a clearly articulated position; reasons for taking that position; answers to likely criticisms; and evidence that you have thought through how the recommendation behaves in a real Codex task.

Do not treat a successful screenshot or a plausible agent message as sufficient evidence. If your argument depends on behavior, say how that behavior would be tested. If it depends on documentation or a practitioner's report, cite the exact source. If you use a run log, diff, benchmark, or test output, explain what it establishes and what alternative explanations remain.

All writing must distinguish your own wording and analysis from borrowed ideas. Quote direct language only when necessary and cite it. Paraphrased ideas require attribution too. External readings retain their own copyright.
